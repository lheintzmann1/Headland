# Asset credits

Every third-party asset must be listed here with its license. CC-BY assets need visible credit in the game.

## Road, track, path and stream tiles

Screaming Brain Studios, CC0 1.0. Converted to top-down atlases by `tools/convert_tiles.py`.
- [700 Isometric Road Tiles](https://opengameart.org/content/700-isometric-road-tiles)
- [5000 Isometric Pathway Tiles](https://opengameart.org/content/5000-isometric-pathway-tiles)

## Font

[Barlow](https://github.com/jpt/barlow) Regular and SemiBold by Jeremy Tribby (The Barlow Project Authors),
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).

## Icons

[Material Symbols](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) (Rounded, filled) by Google, Apache License 2.0
(`icons/LICENSE`). Recolored white so the game can tint them. Weather icons are renamed after the game's weather
conditions: `clear` (sunny), `cloudy` (cloud), `rain` (rainy), `storm` (thunderstorm), `snow` (weather_snowy), `fog`
(foggy). The rest keep their Material Symbols names: `warning`, and the POI map icons `agriculture`, `build`,
`factory`, `home`, `local_gas_station`, `sell`, `storefront`, `warehouse`.

## Crops

Procedurally generated by `tools/gen_crop_cards.py`. Original Headland art, CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Models

Machines (`models/`, `.glb` with a Blockbench `.bbmodel` beside each): simple bases generated from Headland's former
procedural placeholders. Original Headland art, CC BY-SA 4.0. Buildings and the farmer are still procedural
placeholders.
See `docs/MODELING.md` for making models, and add each one below with its author, source and license.
